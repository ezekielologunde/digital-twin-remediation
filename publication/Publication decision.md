# Publication decision and author-review requirements

Prepared 5 October 2026. Nothing has been publicly posted or submitted.

## Current recommendation

Treat the article as a reproducible empirical case study, not a novel security mechanism or validated digital-twin controller. The strongest current evidence concerns action-specific coverage, individual evidence timestamps, controlled counterexamples and conditional event visibility. The experiments are successive small development stages, with shared policy outcomes and deliberately selected faults. Broader workload/fault evaluation and a frozen independent test remain necessary for stronger effectiveness claims. A defensible draft can report these limits; acceptance cannot be promised.

The [Journal of Network and Systems Management](https://link.springer.com/journal/10922/aims-and-scope) is a plausible candidate because its scope includes systems-management case studies. This is a fit recommendation, not a selected venue or assurance of review. The manuscript remains journal-neutral until the author chooses a route. Its [submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/10922/submission-guidelines) should govern the final title page, declarations and formatting. Do not submit overlapping work concurrently to another venue.

For a preprint, follow the [arXiv submission process](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html), including account, author responsibility and any applicable endorsement requirements. A preprint is public dissemination, not peer-reviewed acceptance. No author identity, affiliation or endorsement has been inferred from the pasted research-prompt examples.

## Information needed from the authors

- Exact author names, ordering, affiliations, corresponding-author email and any ORCID identifiers.
- Venue or preprint route, and whether there are existing overlapping manuscripts or submissions.
- Funding, competing interests, contribution statements and institutional requirements.
- Approval of the complete manuscript, the AI-assistance disclosure and the public artifact contents/license.

The package contains the complete empirical narrative and generated results tables, but deliberately leaves authorship/declaration fields unasserted. These fields are not cosmetic: submission must represent the actual authors and their declarations. No claims about funding, conflicts, institutional clearance or novelty should be invented to fill them.

## Release work

Verify a clean LaTeX build; resolve diagnostics or report compilation as unverified. Finalize the title page and declarations. Review source/image provenance and all excluded attempts. Deposit an approved version of the data/code artifact with a persistent identifier, adapting or documenting host-specific bind paths. Insert that identifier into the availability statement. Submit or post only the reviewed final version to the chosen destination. No external message or submission has been sent.
